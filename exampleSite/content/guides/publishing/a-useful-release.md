## Check the purpose

Read the title and opening paragraph together. They should describe what the page helps someone understand or do.

## Follow the links

Open each internal link and check that its label describes the destination. For related reading in this example, visit [Readable pages](/guides/writing/readable-pages/) or the [journal](/journal/).

## Try a narrow screen

Read the page on a small display. Headings should wrap naturally, code should scroll inside its own area, and navigation should remain easy to reach.

## Write a short update

Explain what changed and why a reader might care. The [updates page](/news/) keeps those announcements in one place.
