## Choose a question you can explore today

A useful experiment starts with a question small enough to test. Try a different route on a familiar walk, compare two ways to explain a concept, or measure something you usually estimate.

## Keep a record of what happened

Write down the conditions before recording the result. The details help you distinguish an observation from an assumption.

## Share the useful part

A note about a failed attempt can help another person avoid the same problem. Describe what you would try next and invite a specific suggestion.
