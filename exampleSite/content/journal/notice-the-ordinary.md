## Look again

Choose a familiar place and spend a few minutes describing what is actually there. Notice the light, movement, sound, and small changes you normally pass by.

## Separate observation from interpretation

“The path was wet” records an observation. “It must have rained” is an interpretation. Keeping both makes a notebook more useful.
