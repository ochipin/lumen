## Explain the decision

Record why you chose an approach, including the constraints that mattered. A future reader may face a different situation and need to reconsider it.

## Make the next step concrete

A useful handover names the next action, the material it needs, and the question it should resolve. That is often more helpful than a long account of everything you did.
